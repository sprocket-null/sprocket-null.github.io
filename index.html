hope, small

planned

Google's Nested Learning paper (NeurIPS 2025) proposes a self-modifying architecture called HOPE with a continuum memory system. It reports 1.3B-scale wins. Nobody has shown whether the mechanism survives at 10–30M parameters on one 16 GB card. That is the question I can actually answer.

16 GB VRAM · single GPU · param-matched baseline · kill criteria written in advance

The shippable half. An API model that keeps a working memory across sessions instead of re-reading a transcript. Separate from the research track: no retraining, just a store and a policy for what to surface.

sheaf

hold

Bundling similar images so they compress better together than apart. Measured, and the measurement said no: on real photographs it came out worse than encoding each image alone, and a video codec beat it on the easy case. Parked, not approved for a second pass.

2.46 MiB vs 1.27 MiB for plain JPEG-XL · the negative result is the deliverable
