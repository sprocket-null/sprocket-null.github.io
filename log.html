2026-11-22

Read the Nested Learning paper

Read the paper rather than the blog post. The architecture is a self-modifying sequence model plus a memory system whose blocks update at different frequencies, and the reported results are at 760M and 1.3B parameters. The blog oversells it as unbounded context; the paper is about continual learning and update frequency, and context remains finite.

The thing I keep coming back to is that their own results point in two directions: the perplexity advantage narrows with scale while the accuracy advantage widens. That is either a real crossover or two points of noise, and it is cheap enough to test properly.

2026-11-18

Image bundling: measured and rejected

Built a codec that predicts each image in a group from one anchor image, codes the difference, and reconstructs exactly. On a synthetic burst it reached 1.91 MiB against 1.99 for independent JPEG-XL, and lost to x265 on the same group at 1.72. On six real photographs of one subject it produced 2.46 MiB where independent coding produced 1.27.

The mistake was not the implementation, it was the premise: a video codec already predicts across frames, and one global translation is the wrong model for photographs taken from different positions. The code is still in its repository, marked as parked.
